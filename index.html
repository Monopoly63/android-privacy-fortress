02 · First principles

One boundary is never enough.

A single control — a VPN, a password, a permission prompt — protects one surface and leaves the rest exposed. Real privacy emerges from layers, where each layer constrains a different kind of failure.

01

A VPN alone

Hides traffic from the local network — not your device identifiers, not your metadata, and nothing an installed app already knows about you.

02

Encryption alone

Protects data at rest. It does not stop an unlocked session, a compromised app, or a radio that keeps announcing who you are to the network.

03

Permissions alone

Consent prompts are not isolation. Without sandboxing and mandatory policy, one app's bug becomes every other app's breach.

CONFIDENTIALITYData is readable only by who holds the key.
INTEGRITYSoftware and data remain unmodified and verifiable.
PRIVACYControl over what is revealed, to whom, and when.
ANONYMITYAction without a stable, linkable identity. Distinct from privacy.

        09 · Cellular / baseband

        A second computer in your pocket.

        The cellular modem is its own processor with its own firmware, and it must identify itself to the network. Hardening Android does not make the device invisible to the cellular network — this is an architectural fact, not a configuration oversight.

        ANDROID OS — HARDENED

        • ├── APPS · sandboxed
        • ├── KERNEL · SELinux enforcing
        • └── SYSTEM SERVICES · least privilege

        Controls what software may do on the device.

        COMMANDS ONLY —
        NOT RADIO TRUTH

        CELLULAR MODEM — SEPARATE TRUST DOMAIN

        MODEM↓ CELL TOWER↓ CARRIER

        Controls what the radio says. The OS can ask; it cannot verify.

        • IMEIThe device's radio identity — announced to register on any network.
        • IMSI / SIMSubscriber identity — ties the radio to an account and a person.
        • Cell associationTowers know when you are near, and for how long. That is coarse location, continuously.
        • SignallingRegistration and paging happen even with no apps running at all.

        MITIGATIONS · AIRPLANE MODE (RADIO OFF) · NO-SIM / DISABLED eSIM · TREAT TOR & VPN AS CONTENT PROTECTION, NOT RADIO SILENCE

        Information escapes through behaviour.

          14 · The complete system

          Every layer, one architecture.

          Hardware, boot integrity, isolation, keys, profiles, tunnels, identifiers, the modem, the carrier, backups, monitoring, recovery — assembled.

          Security is not a feature.
          It is an architecture of trust.

          A privacy-focused Android device is not protected by one feature. It is protected by layers of trust, isolation, cryptography, controlled communication, identity separation, and carefully defined boundaries.
